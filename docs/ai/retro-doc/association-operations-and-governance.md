## What it does

This domain describes the association’s operational and governance workflows: attendance and day-of-event operations, inventory, partnerships, voting, executive oversight, legal compliance, document generation, grants, shared planning, GDPR, and auditability. The design record places the main application surfaces under `apps/my-asso`, with tenant data in Firestore, and puts heavier or sensitive legal work in the dedicated `apps/api-juridique` NestJS application. Event-day Carnaval work is integrated into the existing Events area and reuses `RegistrationDayDContext` and `UserContext`.

The record is primarily a set of specifications and implementation plans, not evidence that every proposed subsystem exists. The explicit exceptions are the legal task record, which declares `apps/api-juridique` delivered but is not independently verifiable from the record, and the live user-tracking feature, which is marked implemented.

## Architecture as designed

- **Event participation and operations.** Attendance is designed around tenant-scoped attendance records, signed event-specific QR codes, camera and manual check-in, bulk check-in, statistics, exports, and certificates. The Carnaval “Jour J” area adds Planning and Volunteer Check-in sub-tabs, editable time slots, volunteer assignments, Excel imports, and live present/total feedback. Planning is stored in the `event_planning` collection under a dynamic `carnaval-{year}` event ID; it reuses `RegistrationDayDContext` for mutations and `UserContext` for users. The broader attendance specification proposes `attendance`, `attendanceQRCodes`, `attendanceCertificates`, and `attendanceTemplates`.
- **Inventory and resources.** The inventory design separates equipment catalog data, reservations, loans, returns, maintenance, alerts, evidence, and history. It proposes the `equipment`, `reservations`, `maintenanceLogs`, and `inventoryMovements` collections, with manager and member dashboards and QR confirmation for withdrawals and returns.
- **Relationships and decisions.** Partnership management is divided into partner profiles, contracts, counterparties, periodic visibility reports, and sponsor display configuration. The proposed collections are `partners`, `partnerships`, `counterparties`, `visibilityReports`, and `sponsorDisplayConfig`; reports may be exported as PDF, emailed, and archived in Drive.
- **Voting and governance reporting.** Formal votes support configurable options, secret ballots, quorum, proxies, immutable responses, real-time participation/results, and generated minutes. Informal polls deliberately omit quorum and proxies. The proposed collections are `votes`, `voteResponses`, `proxies`, `polls`, `pollResponses`, and `minutesOfMeeting`. Executive reporting aggregates membership, finance, events, and engagement into KPI dashboards, alerts, forecasts, configurable layouts, and annual reports in `dashboardKPI`, `dashboardAlerts`, `annualReports`, and `dashboardLayouts`.
- **Legal compliance.** `apps/api-juridique` is designed as a separate NestJS service with Firebase Admin through `@linktogo/lk-db`, `ApiKeyGuard`, strict validation, Handlebars/Puppeteer PDF generation, scheduled alerts, and a fail-silent HTTP client for `api-mail`. The data model uses global `legal_templates`, tenant variants in `legal_templates_custom`, and tenant-scoped `legal_obligations`, `legal_deliberations`, `legal_declarations`, `legal_alerts`, and `legal_compliance_audits`. Official document mutations require the bureau roles Président, Secrétaire, or Trésorier; other members are read-only.
- **Documents and grants.** The document generator uses tenant-scoped HTML/CSS templates, typed `{{...}}` variables, PDF/DOCX/HTML output, signatures, bulk generation, encrypted archival, and lifecycle audit records in `documentTemplates`, `generatedDocuments`, `documentSignatures`, and `documentAudits`. The grants module proposes opportunity and application records, a Genkit flow at `src/ai/flows/grant-assistance.ts`, CERFA mappings, a document library, Kanban/timeline collaboration, and `grantOpportunities`, `grantApplications`, `grantDocuments`, and `grantComments`.
- **Planning and privacy.** Advanced shared planning models bookable resources, member availability, on-call schedules, private tokenized iCal feeds, and Google Calendar OAuth. Its proposed collections are `resources`, `bookings`, `memberAvailability`, `onCallSchedules`, and `calendarSyncTokens`. GDPR adds consent, export, deletion, processing-register, cookie, breach, and compliance-dashboard workflows, with collections `consents`, `dataExportRequests`, `deletionRequests`, `dataProcessingRegistry`, `cookieConsents`, and `dataBreaches`.
- **Audit and live support.** The audit design uses global middleware to compare Firestore before/after states, immutable chained-hash records, asynchronous batched writes, search/export, anomaly rules, and encrypted archival in `auditLogs` and `auditLogsArchive`, with `anomalyRules` and `auditReports`. The implemented live-tracking feature uses `src/lib/user-tracking.ts`, `src/contexts/UserTrackingContext.tsx`, `src/components/support-tracking-banner.tsx`, and `/admin/user-tracking`, backed by `user_activity_logs`, `user_presence`, and `support_sessions`.

## Invariants

1. Scope association operational, legal, document, grant, planning, GDPR, audit, attendance, inventory, partnership, voting, and dashboard data by `authGroupId` wherever the model defines tenant data. Source: `specs/004-gestion-presences/spec/spec.md`, `specs/006-gestion-stocks-inventaire/spec/spec.md`, `specs/007-partenariats-sponsors/spec/spec.md`, `specs/008-votes-sondages/spec/spec.md`, `specs/016-generateur-documents/spec/spec.md`, `specs/017-module-subventions/spec/spec.md`, `specs/018-planning-partage-avance/spec/spec.md`, `specs/019-gestion-rgpd/spec/spec.md`, `specs/020-audit-trail/spec/spec.md`.
2. Never allow ordinary users to modify audit logs; logs must remain immutable, and integrity verification must fail on a broken hash chain. Source: `specs/020-audit-trail/spec/spec.md`.
3. Do not expose voter identities for secret votes, and do not allow one member to vote twice through direct and proxy participation. Source: `specs/008-votes-sondages/spec/spec.md`.
4. Keep event rollover non-destructive: do not modify or delete source events, do not copy `Registration` records, and give duplicated events fresh role identifiers and active status. Source: `specs/043-parametres-nouvelle-annee-events/spec/spec.md`; `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-08-events-parametres-nouvelle-annee.md`.
5. Treat events without `fiscalYearId` as legacy “Non classés”; do not backfill them automatically. Source: `specs/043-parametres-nouvelle-annee-events/spec/spec.md`; `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-08-events-parametres-nouvelle-annee.md`.
6. Protect official legal mutations with bureau roles, require `authGroupId` on API operations, and keep external JOAFE, e-Préfecture, Légifrance, and `api-mail` failures non-blocking after retries. Source: `specs/023-module-juridique/spec/spec.md`, `specs/023-module-juridique/quickstart.md`, `specs/023-module-juridique/research.md`.
7. Retain GDPR-governed data for the stated category periods: accounting ten years, tax documents at least six years, payments five years, communications one year unless opposed, and audit logs three years. Source: `specs/019-gestion-rgpd/spec/spec.md`.
8. Expire attendance QR codes 24 hours after the event, audit manual check-ins, retain attendance three years, and retain certificates indefinitely. Source: `specs/004-gestion-presences/spec/spec.md`.
9. Keep the live-tracking exclusions exact: do not record `/login`, `/join`, `/unsubscribe`, or `/api/*`; users may write only their own activity/presence, and only administrators may mutate support sessions or delete activity logs. Source: `specs/044-suivi-live-utilisateur/spec/spec.md`.
10. Keep Jour J Carnaval IDs in the `carnaval-{year}` form and do not conflate them with `soiree-festive-{year}`. Source: `specs/JOUR_J_SYSTEM.md`.

## Drift and superseded decisions

This is the risky part of the document: the design record mixes proposals, plans, and claimed delivery, while the supplied material does not prove that the current code matches every design.

- The legal checklist initially rejected the module as not ready and recommended moving legal watch outside v1. The later legal specification includes all seven subsystems, including legal watch, and the later data model/research rename the initial camelCase collections (`legalDocuments`, `legalObligations`, `deliberations`, `prefectureDeclarations`, `legalAlerts`, `complianceAudits`) to `legal_*` collections. Treat the later data model and research as current, but verify deployed code and rules.
- The legal tasks mark T001–T046 complete and call the backend delivered, yet the plan and quickstart remain implementation-oriented and no deployment evidence is supplied. Do not assume `apps/api-juridique` is deployed.
- The event-year plan and specification agree on `Event.date` as free text and explicitly reject automatic date parsing/shifting. Any implementation that derives new dates automatically would contradict the current decision.
- The Jour J document marks absence notifications, PDF export, attendance statistics, real-time synchronization, mobile app support, and QR check-in as future work. Do not infer those capabilities from the attendance specification.
- The attendance, inventory, partnership, voting, executive-dashboard, document, grants, planning, GDPR, and audit documents are specifications with phased delivery plans and no implementation status. Their collections and routes are proposed architecture, not confirmed runtime surfaces.
- The live-tracking specification and tasks explicitly say implemented, unlike the other operational specifications. Even there, the console-guard test is a required synchronization safeguard; verify rules and index diagnostics before changing tracking behavior.

## Open questions

- Which of the proposed attendance, inventory, partnership, voting, executive-dashboard, document-generation, grants, advanced-planning, GDPR, and audit surfaces are actually shipped, and what are their current paths and collection names?
- Is `apps/api-juridique` deployed and reachable in the intended environment, and do deployed collections use the later `legal_*` names rather than the initial names?
- What exact association configuration document should hold `eventYearMode` and `activeEventFiscalYearId`? The event-year specification leaves this location open.
- What exact label and placement should the Events settings card use? The event-year specification leaves that UI decision open.
- Are legal bureau roles represented consistently in claims, `user_profiles`, or another authorization source? The record specifies role names and guards but does not settle the storage contract.
- Which attendance certificate and document templates are authoritative when configurable templates, localized templates, and generated PDFs overlap?
- What retention, archival, and deletion implementation is currently enforced for the proposed GDPR and audit periods?
- Has a real PDP partner been selected for any association-facing electronic-invoicing work? The supplied legal sources do not define one; this domain should not assume PDP integration.
