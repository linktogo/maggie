## What it does

This domain covers communication between association members, outbound email campaigns, association-owned public portals and blog content, and Linktogo’s public marketing site. The supplied records describe these as distinct surfaces: internal messaging and notifications in MyAsso; email campaigns under Communications; public association pages and articles; a blog API; and the deployed Angular marketing app at `apps/landing-next`.

The records establish design intent and some delivery status, but do not establish that every proposed feature or redesign is live. Treat specifications and plans as design evidence, not as proof of current runtime behavior.

## Architecture as designed

- **Internal messaging and notifications:** `specs/005-messagerie-notifications-push/spec/spec.md` proposes private and group conversations, push subscriptions and preferences, an in-app notification center, and scheduled reminders. Its intended pieces include `MessagingContext`, `NotificationContext`, messaging and notification services, API routes, and a service worker. This is a feature specification; it does not report implementation status.
- **Email campaigns:** `specs/026-module-email-campagnes/spec/spec.md` and its plan place campaign management under `/communications/email/`, with `/email` retained as a redirect and `/unsubscribe` as a public page. Campaigns build on the existing SMTP/nodemailer email service, use Unlayer for HTML composition, and are sent through the existing email foundations. Firestore stores campaigns, per-campaign logs, lists and their contacts, and unsubscribe records; contacts and logs are subcollections rather than arrays on parent documents. A scheduled Next.js route claims a campaign transactionally before sending it.
- **Public association portal:** `specs/013-portail-public/spec/spec.md` proposes tenant-specific public routes at `/public/[authGroupId]/`, built with Next.js App Router, static generation and ISR. An association configures pages, theme, navigation and SEO through a no-code builder; the public content model includes articles, events, galleries, team and documents, alongside an online membership flow.
- **Blog API:** `specs/024-migration-blog-api-firebase/spec/spec.md` proposes moving the NestJS blog API from Cloudflare Workers to Firebase Cloud Functions without changing its Firestore data model or endpoints. The plan wraps and caches the NestJS application in a function in `europe-west1`; staging verification precedes production traffic redirection. Its task list does not record the migration as completed.
- **Linktogo marketing site:** The redesign records identify `apps/landing-next` as the deployed Angular marketing site, distinct from the deprecated Next.js `apps/my-asso-landing`. The proposed page is composed from Hero, Features, AI, Screenshots, Pricing and Integrations sections, with handwritten SCSS and four aligned locales. The subsequent header design adds a logo mark and a standalone locale switcher while retaining the navigation structure.

These surfaces depend on shared authentication, tenant identity, localization, email delivery, and external-service configuration, but the supplied records do not define those cross-domain implementations in detail.

## Drift and superseded decisions

This is the risky part of the document: the supplied records mix approved designs, implementation plans and task lists, and several plans explicitly remain unchecked. Do not infer that a planned route, control, migration or screen exists merely because it is described here.

- The 2026-08-11 header design is later than the 2026-08-10 landing redesign. Keep the earlier navigation structure and labels, but use the later custom locale dropdown instead of a native selector. The redesign and header plan checklists are unchecked, so this does not prove that either change has shipped.
- The landing redesign names `apps/landing-next` as the deployed marketing site and rejects the deprecated `apps/my-asso-landing`; its proposed content changes are not documented as completed.
- The blog migration tasks in `specs/024-migration-blog-api-firebase/spec/tasks.md` are all unchecked. The Cloud Functions architecture is the selected design, not evidence of a completed cutover.
- The email campaign analysis (`specs/026-module-email-campagnes/spec/analysis.md`) evaluates the V2 design as coherent, but the plan and task records still say “Not Started.” The feature is not established as implemented.
- The campaign documents disagree on the public unsubscribe URL shape: the specification includes a `token` form in one user story but specifies `lid`, `exp` and `sig` elsewhere. The analysis also flags unresolved HMAC secret rotation/versioning, signed-link lifetime, auto-save, and whether unsubscribe scope is tenant-wide. Do not choose a URL contract or change its security scope without resolving these points.
- The marketing site pricing decisions in the redesign are specific: the Association plan is free for loi 1901 nonprofits, Independent is 10 € HT/month/user, and both calls to action go to the existing demo at `https://tools.linktogo.fr`; no free trial is offered. Do not replace these with generalized pricing copy.

The messaging and portal specifications provide no implementation status; the landing redesign and header tasks are unchecked; the campaign and blog migration tasks remain unchecked.

- **Unsubscribe contract:** Which public URL parameter form is authoritative (`token` or `lid`/`exp`/`sig`), what is the signed-link lifetime, how are signing secrets rotated, and is an unsubscribe scoped to one association or shared more broadly?
