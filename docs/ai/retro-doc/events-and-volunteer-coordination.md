## What it does

Events and Volunteer Coordination covers association event records and calendars, fiscal-year organization, volunteer registrations, day-of planning and attendance, and individual event-photo production. The design record places the principal MyAsso surfaces under `apps/my-asso/src/app/(main)/events/`, with shared models and helpers in `apps/my-asso/src/lib/`, state in contexts under `apps/my-asso/src/contexts/`, and registration writes in `apps/api-event/src/events/events.service.ts`. Manual calendar events also have a public read endpoint in `apps/api-event`; the day-of Carnaval record describes a separate `event_planning` collection.

The record describes both established patterns and proposed or implemented features. In particular, the dated multi-day calendar design and photo-production task record say they are implemented; several other sources are specs or plans and do not establish current implementation status.

## Architecture as designed

- Agenda `Event` records are distinct from manual calendar entries. Agenda events may have an optional `fiscalYearId`; manual entries are stored separately in Firestore's `calendar_events` collection because they do not use volunteer-registration fields. Manual entries belong to an `authGroupId`.
- Year settings reuse `FiscalYear`, with association-level `eventYearMode` (`civil` or `scolaire`) and `activeEventFiscalYearId`. The July plan locates settings in `feature_configs/{authGroupId}` through `FeatureConfigContext`; event-year calculations and filtering belong in the pure `apps/my-asso/src/lib/event-year.ts` helper. The proposed UI is `/events/parametres`, `/events/parametres/nouvelle-annee`, and a year selector on `/events/list`.
- Rollover is a review-and-duplicate flow for selected agenda events, not a destructive replacement. It creates a fiscal year, then duplicates selected events into it. The original records and their registrations are left intact; duplicates start without registrations. Event dates are free text, so users edit each proposed date rather than relying on automatic shifting.
- Volunteer registrations are linked to their event and carry a denormalized `fiscalYearId`. The `apps/api-event/src/events/events.service.ts` write path takes the year from the already-read event snapshot; `apps/my-asso/src/contexts/EventContext.tsx` normalizes missing values to `null`. `/events/volontear` scopes its list, chart, unique-volunteer count, event choices, and exports to the selected year. A manual script, `scripts/backfill-registration-fiscal-year.ts`, was planned for existing records.
- Manual calendar CRUD is performed directly from the frontend through `CalendarEventContext`; the design adds no HTTP create, update, or delete routes. `apps/api-event` exposes only the public `GET /calendar-events?authGroupId=...` feed, which returns manual events as a raw array without tenant IDs or storage timestamps.
- Multi-day manual calendar periods are represented by one `calendar_events` document per inclusive date, linked by a shared `periodId`. `calendar-event-period.ts` handles date expansion and sibling lookup; the calendar page orchestrates grouped creation, edits, and deletion while context methods remain single-document operations. Keep single-day edits in place; replace documents when editing a period or creating a multi-day range. The public feed consequently returns one entry per day, not an aggregated period.
- Day-of coordination has two separate described designs. `specs/JOUR_J_SYSTEM.md` describes Carnaval Planning and Volunteer Check-in tabs using `event_planning` records keyed with `eventId` values of `carnaval-{year}`; the attendance feature spec separately proposes QR, manual, and list check-in, attendance records, statistics, sheets, and certificates. The latter does not report implementation status.
- Individual photo production is located in `apps/my-asso/src/app/(main)/events/photo-individuelle/page.tsx`. The described workflow reads source images from Drive, calls `/api/photos-personnalise/process-image`, uploads processed WebP images to a `Traitées` folder, and creates a client-side ZIP organized by time slot or participant, with unmatched photos in `Non classées/`. The task record describes parallel image processing and per-image failure isolation; the earlier design plan calls for sequential processing.
- `specs/018-planning-partage-avance/spec/spec.md` proposes a broader shared-planning area for resources, bookings, member availability, on-call schedules, and external calendars. Its relationship to the event and volunteer flows above is not specified.

## Invariants

1. Keep `Event.fiscalYearId` optional/nullable. Do not backfill legacy agenda events automatically; missing or null event years remain filterable as unclassified. Source: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-08-events-parametres-nouvelle-annee.md`; `specs/043-parametres-nouvelle-annee-events/spec/spec.md`.
2. A year selector changes the view only. Do not delete or archive events when changing the active year. Source: `specs/043-parametres-nouvelle-annee-events/spec/spec.md`.
3. Do not mutate or delete prior-year events during rollover, and do not copy their `registrations`. If fiscal-year creation fails, do not duplicate any events; if an individual duplication fails, continue with the remaining selections and report actual successes and failures. Source: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-08-events-parametres-nouvelle-annee.md`; `specs/043-parametres-nouvelle-annee-events/spec/spec.md`.
4. Keep registration classification on the registration itself. Set it from the linked event when creating the registration, normalize missing stored values to `null` on read, and let the unclassified filter match both null and absent legacy values. Source: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-15-benevoles-annee-fiscale-design.md`; `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-08-15-benevoles-annee-fiscale.md`.
5. Apply the selected fiscal-year filter before volunteer-page search, event filtering, summaries, and export. Do not add an “All years” option; retain the unclassified option and default to the active event year. Source: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-15-benevoles-annee-fiscale-design.md`.
6. Scope every manual `calendar_events` record to an `authGroupId`. Do not expose `authGroupId`, `createdAt`, or `updatedAt` in the public feed; omit unset `endTime` and `benevoleUrl` fields rather than returning them as null. Source: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-10-calendar-manual-events-design.md`; `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-10-calendar-manual-events.md`.
7. Limit manual calendar categories to `fete`, `vente`, `reunion`, `kermesse`, and `autre`; keep manual events separate from volunteer events and their registration workflow. Source: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-10-calendar-manual-events-design.md`.
8. For a multi-day manual event, use inclusive `"YYYY-MM-DD"` dates and one shared `periodId`; single-day events have no `periodId`. Apply the same entered times to every day. Keep the one-document-to-one-displayed-event mapping and do not aggregate days in the public API. Source: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-calendar-multi-day-periods-design.md`.
9. Do not send optional fields to Firestore as `undefined`; omit them on create and use `deleteField()` when an update removes an optional value. Keep grouped multi-document period operations sequential and treat them as non-atomic. Source: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-calendar-multi-day-periods-design.md`; `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-13-calendar-multi-day-periods.md`.
10. Block rollover submission when no events are selected, when a new-year or replacement event date is invalid, or while submission is in progress. Permit only `Admin`, `Editor`, and `Gestion` to use event-year settings and rollover. Source: `specs/043-parametres-nouvelle-annee-events/spec/spec.md`; `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-08-events-parametres-nouvelle-annee.md`.
11. Keep the manual calendar UI's form and detail copy in French as designed, with the page-level add-event key in all four locales. For year settings and rollover, provide translated user-facing strings in French, English, German, and Spanish. Do not infer a single localization rule for these separate surfaces. Source: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-10-calendar-manual-events-design.md`; `specs/043-parametres-nouvelle-annee-events/spec/spec.md`.
12. In individual photo production, continue after an individual image failure and keep processed blobs in memory for the workflow. Put unmatchable photos in `Non classées/`; use `Traitées` for processed Drive uploads. Source: `specs/029-photo-production-tabs/spec/plan.md`; `specs/029-photo-production-tabs/spec/spec.md`; `specs/029-photo-production-tabs/spec/tasks.md`.
13. For any attendance implementation based on the attendance spec, validate QR tokens server-side, expire them 24 hours after the event, invalidate an old QR when regenerating, and retain the check-in method, time, operator, and status. Audit manual check-ins and enforce `authGroupId` boundaries. Source: `specs/004-gestion-presences/spec/spec.md`.

## Drift and superseded decisions

This is the risky part of the document: the record mixes dated plans, approved designs, undated specs, and task checklists. A design marked implemented or a checked task list is not a substitute for checking the current code and deployed Firestore configuration.

- The July event-year plan selects `feature_configs/{authGroupId}` and `FeatureConfigContext` for the association's mode and active-year pointer. The later undated feature spec still leaves the exact association-config location undecided. Confirm the persisted field names and location before changing settings storage.
- The year plan and rollover spec agree that `Event.date` is free text; do not apply calendar arithmetic to copied agenda-event dates. This is a confirmed limitation, not an implementation omission to “fix” without a date-model decision.
- The 2026-08-15 volunteer plan calls for an idempotent manual registration backfill after the write-path change, but explicitly does not claim it was run against real data. Existing production registrations may therefore remain unclassified; do not assume the task list proves migration completion.
- The 2026-09-10 manual-event design allows only Admin, Editor, and Gestion to manage entries in the UI. The 2026-09-13 multi-day design records an unresolved Firestore delete-rule mismatch: the UI permits Editor/Gestion, while the documented Firestore rule allows only Admin to delete. Verify the active rule before changing edit/delete behavior.
- The initial multi-day design had no duration maximum and proposed deleting the old period before creating replacements. The later reviewed design reverses both: cap ranges at 62 days and create replacements first, deleting old records only after all replacements succeed.
- The 2026-09-10 manual-event spec is marked approved but pending implementation; the 2026-09-13 multi-day spec is marked implemented. The latter does not by itself prove that the original manual-event CRUD, feed, and rules have shipped.
- Photo-production records have different workflow versions. `specs/003-photos-personnalise/plan-traitement-image.md` proposes a seventh “Traitement” tab on `events/photo-individuelle` and sequential processing, while `specs/029-photo-production-tabs` describes a three-tab workflow and a completed task list that marks parallel processing complete. The 003 plan also concerns a different page from the five-tab `events/photos-personnalise` feature in `specs/003-photos-personnalise/tasks.md`; the relationship is not established. Verify the actual pages and workflow before adding or renaming tabs.
- The photo-production quality checklist described ZIP download as the remaining feature, while the later 029 spec and tasks report the ZIP and the full workflow implemented. Prefer the later completion record for design history, but check the actual route and tests before relying on it.
- `specs/JOUR_J_SYSTEM.md` describes Carnaval's `event_planning` integration and possible future attendance features. It labels absence notifications, PDF export, attendance statistics, real-time sync, dedicated mobile check-in, and QR check-in as future possibilities; do not treat them as shipped features.
- The attendance and advanced shared-planning documents are specifications without implementation status. Attendance retention, offline sync, resource-booking workflows, and external calendar synchronization are requirements/proposals in those records, not proof of current behavior.
